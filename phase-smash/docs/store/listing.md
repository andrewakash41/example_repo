## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Tap, hold, smash. Phase Smash is a one-thumb 3D arcade smasher with a twist you
won't find in other helix games: your ball keeps switching color, and you can
only smash the segments that match.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Hold to smash down the glowing tower. Release to bounce and wait.
• Your ball flips between amber and azure on a timer. Match the color to break
  through — hit the opposite color and you bounce back.
• Dodge the deadly obsidian segments... unless you're in Fever.
• Chain 10 smashes to ignite FEVER and blast through everything.

FEATURES
• 50 hand-tuned levels, then endless procedural towers that never stop.
• Boss towers with independently spinning bands every 10 levels.
• 12 unlockable ball skins and 3 boosters (Shield, Slow-Mo, Head Start).
• Free crates with every clear.
• Buttery 60fps, built to run great on everyday phones.
• Plays fully offline. No account, no sign-in.

One touch. One rule: smash only what matches. How deep can you go?

Phase Smash is free to play and supported by ads. A one-time consent screen
appears on first launch where required.
```
