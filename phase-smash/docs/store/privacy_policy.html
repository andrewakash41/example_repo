Privacy Policy — Phase Smash

Last updated: [DATE]

Phase Smash ("the game", "we") is a free, ad-supported mobile game. This policy explains what data is collected and why. We keep it deliberately minimal.

Data the game itself collects

None that leaves your device. All progress (levels, scores, skins, boosters, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate accounts, sign-in, cloud saves, or analytics servers.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as device identifiers and ad-interaction information. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required (EEA/UK and other regions), a consent screen appears on first launch via Google's User Messaging Platform. If you decline personalized ads, ads are shown in a non-personalized form. You can revisit this choice any time via Settings → Privacy options.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. You can change ad-consent via the in-game privacy options, and reset your Advertising ID in your device settings.
